What this version does

Local EDF/EDF+ and CSV import, offline replay, spectral exploration, heuristic quality flags, baseline comparisons, and persistent annotations.

What remains to be validated

Deviation scores and quality thresholds are exploratory heuristics. They have not been validated for disease detection, symptom measurement, or wearable deployment. Session-to-session comparison, live hardware streaming, and audio/movement integration are future work.

Import a recording

EDF / EDF+ or channel-only CSV. Up to 64 MB and 12 million values. At least 24 seconds.

CSV: first row contains channel names; all following rows are numeric samples. No time column. EDF units and sampling rate are read from its header.

The file is sent only to the app running on this computer.

Add an observation

Read the signal, then add context.

Reference interval. Select at least 12 seconds. Each channel needs five unflagged 4-second windows. Overlapping windows are not independent measurements.

Deviation score. We compare log band power with the reference median, scaled by median absolute deviation. The largest difference across eligible channels and bands is shown. A score of 4 is a heuristic threshold, not a medical cutoff or probability.

Quality comes first. Flat samples, large amplitudes, abrupt jumps, and strong mains interference are flagged on the raw signal. Flagged channels are excluded from that window's score. These checks can miss artifacts and can also flag real activity.

Frequency bands. Delta 1–4, theta 4–8, alpha 8–13, beta 13–30 Hz. Band names describe frequency ranges, not unique mental states. Muscle activity and eye movements can overlap with EEG.

Offline processing. Zero-phase filtering uses future samples. Playback simulates viewing a recording; it is not a validated real-time detector.

Privacy. No telemetry or external scripts. The PhysioNet button makes an explicit download. Your imported signals, notes, and exports stay local unless you share them.
